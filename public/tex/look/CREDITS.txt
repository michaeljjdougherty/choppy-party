Surface detail textures (look/ and look512/)

Made for Choppy Party from scratch by tools/gentex.py (procedural noise, no source images),
so they are our own and free to use (treat as CC0 1.0).

Each JPEG packs: R = height (bump), G = albedo detail (0.5 = neutral), B = roughness variation.
grass, dirt, cobble, rock, wood, metal, snow, tiles, brick, bark, fabric, toy, sand.

Regenerate:  python3 tools/gentex.py public/tex/look 1024 && python3 tools/gentex.py public/tex/look512 512
