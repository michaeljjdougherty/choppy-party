## Tips for the low-poly look

These are photo-scanned, so on Kenney-style models they work best when softened: tint them with the material `color`, keep `repeat` low (2 to 4 on a floor), and use the normal map at a low `normalScale` (around 0.3 to 0.5). For a flatter look, use only the normal map on a solid-colored material.
